ABOUT THE SHOP

A neighbourhood tailor, trusted for the big days.

K.K. Tailor has built its name in Borivali West on one simple idea: a suit should fit the person, not just the pattern. Run personally by Alpeshbhai, the shop stays on top of current styles while keeping the classic hand-finishing that makes a garment last.

Families come back year after year — for a daughter's wedding trousseau, a son's first tuxedo, or a father's everyday formal wear. Fabric is available in-store, so you can choose, discuss and get measured in a single visit.
